## 1. Status

Treat retailer compensation as a **high-priority classification problem**.

Do **not** automatically classify it as CONTROLLABLE ADMINISTRATION or UNAVOIDABLE DIRECT ECONOMIC COST.

**Preserve as: CLASSIFICATION REQUIRED.**

## 2. Later determination (not in this CWC)

- economic purpose;
- statutory basis;
- contractual basis;
- competitive-market necessity;
- amount/rate;
- whether physical distribution requires it;
- whether digital distribution changes it;
- whether compensation can be competitively redesigned.

Apply WD-BILL-A-167. Do not invent an amount, rate, or Future-State commission. Do not treat current retailer compensation as a Future-State entitlement (WD-BILL-A-168) or erase it to manufacture 90/10 (WD-BILL-A-169).
