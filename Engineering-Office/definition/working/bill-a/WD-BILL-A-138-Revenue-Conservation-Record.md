## 1. Lottery conservation

If 4% of post-prize revenue enters individual Rule-of-72 partitions, that value comes from **post-prize governmental remainder** unless later evidence establishes another real source.

| Flow | Recipient | Balance-sheet effect |
|---|---|---|
| Gross lottery sales | Lottery operator / state scheme (current-state) | Cash in |
| Prize obligations | Prize winners | Cash out / prize liability |
| 4% of post-prize (planning) | Individual partition | Individual **asset**; **not** remaining free governmental revenue |
| Remaining post-prize after partition | Governmental use **if authorized** | Governmental revenue / restricted purpose |

**Forbidden double count:** the same dollar as **full governmental revenue** and **individually owned capital**.

If the individual later holds a public-debt instrument:

| Party | Effect |
|---|---|
| Individual | Asset (debt claim) |
| Issuer | Cash **and** liability |

## 2. Other toolbox conservation notes

- Local envelope dollars cannot also be counted as leftover PT.  
- TRR coverage of an obligation does not also create a separate ARR claim for the same obligation.  
- Intergovernmental pass-through is not additional Kansas compulsory authority.  
- GCD-degrading claims cannot be treated as a permanent replacement base.
