## 1. Preserved candidate distinction (WD-BILL-A-152)

**PROPERTY-TAX ABATEMENT** = reducing a property-tax requirement that otherwise would remain **during transition**.

**PROPERTY-TAX RETIREMENT** = permanently extinguishing an identified portion of remaining property-tax **authority** or an associated obligation where legally and fiscally possible.
