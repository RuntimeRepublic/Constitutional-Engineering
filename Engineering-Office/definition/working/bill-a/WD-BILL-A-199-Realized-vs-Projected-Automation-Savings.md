## 1. Distinction

Preserve the aggressive automation objective (WD-BILL-A-151 / WD-BILL-A-176).

Only **realized** savings that actually reduce controllable administrative cost affect the measured cash waterfall.

```text
CURRENT ADMIN COST
→ REDESIGN / AUTOMATION
→ VERIFIED FUTURE COST
→ REALIZED COST REDUCTION
→ INCREASED AVAILABLE GOVERNMENTAL CASH.
```

Do **not** book hypothetical AI savings as current revenue. Projected savings ≠ 90-side cash until actually realized in the financial system (WD-BILL-A-188).
