## 1. Candidate Definition

**AVAILABLE GOVERNMENTAL CASH** = actual monetary value remaining within the defined 90/10 engineering base that is lawfully and economically available for governmental allocation after all prior recognized waterfall claims have been accounted for.

The final legal definition remains subject to research and later legislative engineering.

## 2. Forbidden counts

The definition SHALL NOT count:

- the Rule-of-72 partition a second time;
- unrealized economic value;
- projected savings not actually realized;
- hypothetical automation savings;
- accounting credits without cash;
- taxpayer telemetry itself;
- NBBF performance value;
- avoided future costs unless converted into an actual fiscal effect;
- debt proceeds without corresponding liability recognition;
- the same cash in multiple destinations.

## 3. Relation to Q-012

Remaining available governmental lottery cash remains intended **primarily** for property-tax abatement/retirement, subject to protected obligations. That destination does **not** convert unavailable cash into available cash.
