## 1. Ten-question test (apply per material dollar-class later)

1. Is the dollar already required to satisfy a valid **prize obligation**?  
2. Is the dollar subject to a **binding contractual obligation**?  
3. Is the dollar subject to a **constitutional requirement**?  
4. Is the dollar subject to another **legally protected obligation** that Bill A cannot impair?  
5. Is the expenditure **economically necessary** to operate the lottery safely and lawfully?  
6. Is the cost merely a **current administrative practice** that may be redesigned?  
7. Is the destination created **only by current statute** and therefore potentially subject to legislative redesign?  
8. Is the same dollar already allocated to the **individual Rule-of-72 partition**?  
9. Would reallocating the dollar cause **double counting** elsewhere?  
10. Is **additional evidence** required before classification?

## 2. Candidate outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning (Definition-level) |
|---|---|
| **PROTECTED** | Legally required; Bill A cannot impair |
| **UNAVOIDABLE** | Economically necessary to operate the lottery safely and lawfully (evidence required) |
| **CONTROLLABLE** | Administrative practice that may be redesigned |
| **POTENTIALLY REALLOCABLE** | Created primarily by current statute; redesign may be possible after audit |
| **AVAILABLE** | After prizes, partition, and evidenced protected/unavoidable claims; candidate for PT abatement/retirement |
| **UNKNOWN / RESEARCH REQUIRED** | Default until evidence exists |

Prize-required dollars are **not** available. Partition dollars are **not** simultaneously available governmental revenue (WD-BILL-A-154). Current statutory allocation **alone** does not define Bill A’s intended Future State. Protected obligations **must not** be pretended away.
