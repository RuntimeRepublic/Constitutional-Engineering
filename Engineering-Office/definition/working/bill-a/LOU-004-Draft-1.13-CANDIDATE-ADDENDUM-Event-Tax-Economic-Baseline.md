## B. New Human Engineering Intent (research requirement)

Bill A SHALL maintain Kansas-specific quantitative research sufficient to explain the economic scale of replacing compulsory taxes—particularly property taxation—with an event-based final-consumption tax architecture.

Research SHALL address:

1. Kansas aggregate taxable-consumption / sales-use-tax economics;  
2. Kansas transaction-size distribution **where defensible evidence exists**;  
3. Attribution of current sales/use-tax revenue to transaction-size bands **where evidence exists**;  
4. Whether U.S. Treasury, IRS, KDOR, Census, BLS, Federal Reserve, or other authoritative sources possess data capable of answering the transaction-band question;  
5. Event-tax rates that would generate specified revenue amounts, including amounts comparable to current Kansas property-tax quantities;  
6. The distinction between **CURRENT-STATE REVENUE EQUIVALENCE** and **BILL A’S FUTURE AUTHORIZED REVENUE REQUIREMENT**.
