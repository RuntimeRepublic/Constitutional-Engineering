## 2. Public-debt objective (Human Intent)

Qualifying personal partitions may ultimately hold or reference **authorized public-debt obligations**.

**Not selected in this CWC:** TreasuryDirect; Treasury bills/notes/bonds; special issues; trusts; pooled funds; custodians; beneficial ownership; state debt; federal debt; maturity; liquidity; inheritance treatment; tax treatment.

Those remain later legal/financial engineering questions (WD-BILL-A-123 LOT-G-006–008, LOT-G-021).

## 3. Conservation if a debt instrument is later used

If a public-debt instrument allows government to receive cash while the individual receives a debt asset:

```text
INDIVIDUAL → ASSET (authorized public-debt claim)
DEBT ISSUER → CASH + LIABILITY
```

Do **not** describe debt issuance as creation of free wealth.

## 4. Separation from NBBF clawback

The individual partition arises from the qualifying transaction under the proposed architecture.  
Clawback/redirection arises from separately engineered accountability conditions.  
Do not merge rights, balances, triggers, or accounting.
