## 1. Destination (Q-BILL-A-012 = A)

Available lottery governmental revenue → property-tax abatement / retirement → reduction in remaining property-tax requirement. Property tax still → **ZERO statewide**. Lottery may **accelerate**. Lottery has **not** been shown sufficient by itself.

## 2. Candidate Definition distinction (later reconciliation)

**ABATEMENT** = reducing the amount of property-tax revenue that otherwise would be required **during the transition**.

**RETIREMENT** = permanently extinguishing an identified portion of remaining property-tax **authority** or an associated obligation when legally and fiscally possible.

This distinction is **candidate Definition language**. It is **not** operative legal text. Ranking/sequencing of abatement vs retirement is **not** decided in this CWC.

## 3. Relation to 5/7 maximum runways

Maximum runways remain maximums, not collection entitlements. Available lottery revenue, if later evidenced, may increase the acceleration already authorized in CWC-CE-146. No Year-1 formula is invented here.
