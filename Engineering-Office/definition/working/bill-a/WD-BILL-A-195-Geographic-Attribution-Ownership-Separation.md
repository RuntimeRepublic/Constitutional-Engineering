## 1. Preserved Human Intent (attribution)

**Identified Kansas purchaser:** 50% → county of sale; 50% → purchaser's Kansas county of domicile. If identical: effective attribution = 100% to that county.

**Anonymous purchase:** 50% → county of sale; 50% → Kansas 105-County Fund.

Unresolved: 105-County Fund distribution formula; identified non-Kansas purchaser treatment. Do **not** invent either.

## 2. Ownership firewall

GEOGRAPHIC ATTRIBUTION ≠ COUNTY-GOVERNMENT OWNERSHIP.

AVAILABLE GOVERNMENTAL CASH may be geographically attributed without granting the county government an unrestricted property interest in that cash.

**MONEY FOLLOWS LEGITIMATE AUTHORIZED FUNCTION, NOT HISTORICAL LEVYING ENTITY.**

This is especially important for USDs, counties, cities, townships, special districts, debt obligations, and other authorized local functions. Do **not** establish allocation formulas.
