## Letter text (for Human transmission)

Subject: Request for aggregated Kansas taxable sales/use information by transaction-dollar-size bands — research only; no confidential taxpayer records

Dear Office of Policy and Research:

I am requesting **aggregated statistical information** to support nonpartisan legislative-engineering research on Kansas tax architecture. This is **not** an audit, enforcement, or individual-taxpayer request.

I do **not** request personally identifiable taxpayer information; individual retailer tax returns; individual consumer records; confidential taxpayer records; payment-card identities; bank-account information; or individual transaction identities.

### Research purpose

Public Kansas sources describe **aggregate** sales/use collections. They do **not** (so far as our research has located) report **taxable retail transactions by dollar-size band**. The practical research question is:

**What does Kansas actually collect from taxable transactions at different transaction sizes**, including around $20, $30, $100, and progressively larger amounts?

Purchase arithmetic (for example, $30 × a tax rate) is **not** a substitute for observed revenue from transactions in that range.

This request concerns the **current Kansas sales/use system**. It does **not** ask the Department to define any future tax base, future rate, or any Bill A / H.R. 25-style consumption base.

A comparison context for the research is current Kansas general property-tax magnitude on the order of **$6.667 billion** for FY 2025 under the Kansas Legislative Research Department “General Property / levied for collection during the fiscal year” definition. That figure is a **comparison model**, not a claim that any future system must replace that amount dollar-for-dollar.

### Primary question

Does the Kansas Department of Revenue possess data that can report, or can be aggregated to report, **Kansas taxable retail transactions by transaction-dollar-size bands** for a defined period?

- If **yes**: what aggregated transaction counts and taxable-dollar totals can KDOR provide?  
- If **no**: what is the **lowest available level of aggregation** that would permit responsible estimation of the distribution?

Acceptable answers include: exact aggregate available; different aggregate available; partial; not maintained; not public; confidential; unknown / referred.

### Preferred analytical bands (not proposed tax brackets)

These are **receipt / transaction-size research bands**, not tax brackets and not a proposed rate schedule:

| Band ID | Inclusive range |
|---|---|
| B01 | $0.00–$20.00 |
| B02 | $20.01–$30.00 |
| B03 | $30.01–$50.00 |
| B04 | $50.01–$100.00 |
| B05 | $100.01–$250.00 |
| B06 | $250.01–$500.00 |
| B07 | $500.01–$1,000.00 |
| B08 | $1,000.01+ |

If these exact bands cannot be produced, please identify **any standard transaction-size groupings** already used internally or in existing reports.

Please preserve particular visibility around **$20**, **$30**, **$100**, and larger transactions if any grouping can do so.

### Ideal fields per band (provide only what exists)

For each band, if available:

1. Number of taxable transactions  
2. Total gross transaction dollars  
3. Total taxable transaction dollars  
4. State sales tax collected  
5. State use tax collected (where applicable)  
6. Local sales tax collected  
7. Local use tax collected (where applicable)  
8. Total sales/use tax collected  
9. Number of retailers represented  
10. Period covered  
11. Whether the data represent point-of-sale receipts or tax-return aggregates  
12. Known exclusions  
13. Data-quality, rounding, and suppression notes  

Please do not invent fields. “Not maintained” for a field is a useful answer.

Please distinguish, where possible: **gross sales; taxable sales; exempt sales; sales tax; use tax; state tax; local tax.**

### Geographic resolution

Statewide Kansas data are sufficient for the first research model. If available, please state whether the same aggregates can be produced for county, city, ZIP code, taxing jurisdiction, or another official geographic unit. County-level availability is **helpful**, not a condition of usefulness.

### Time resolution

Please use the most useful recent **complete** calendar year **or** fiscal year, and identify the exact period. Please do not mix fiscal and calendar periods without disclosure.

If equivalent data exist for additional years, those would help evaluate cycles, inflation, behavior, food-tax changes, remote-sales changes, and volatility.

### Reporting unit (critical)

Please state whether Kansas sales/use reporting captures:

- individual receipts / transactions;  
- aggregated retailer taxable sales for a filing period;  
- tax-return totals;  
- point-of-sale information;  
- marketplace-facilitator or remote-seller totals;  
- or another reporting unit.

If the Department receives **period-aggregate tax-return amounts** rather than receipt-level records, please say so. That architectural limitation is itself useful evidence. Please do not force a receipt-size distribution that the underlying reports cannot support.

### Payment processors / third-party systems

If the Department receives or maintains **aggregated** information from payment processors, marketplace facilitators, remote sellers, or electronic filing systems that includes transaction counts or transaction-size information, please describe that capability at the aggregate level only. No protected individual records are requested.

### If exact bands are unavailable

Please identify the **closest available** dataset rather than ending at “not available,” for example:

- transaction counts;  
- average or median taxable transaction size;  
- percentiles;  
- industry-level (NAICS) taxable sales;  
- retailer-size distributions;  
- county or city taxable sales;  
- marketplace-facilitator totals;  
- remote-sales totals;  
- taxable-services totals;  
- or other official aggregates useful for modeling.

### Definitions and methodology requested

Please include, as applicable: data definitions; period definitions; taxability rules applied; treatment of food versus general merchandise; in-store versus remote / marketplace; rounding and suppression; known exclusions; and any confidentiality or use limitations that apply to the aggregates provided.

### Classification of the response

It would assist later research if the Department can indicate whether the product is official agency data, an official agency explanation, an agency estimate, not maintained, not disclosable, or requires a different request form.
