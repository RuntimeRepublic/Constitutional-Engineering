## 1. Requirement

Treat multi-state games separately from Kansas-only products where necessary.

Identify later:

- MUSL or equivalent obligations;
- prize-pool mechanics;
- vendor obligations;
- required contributions;
- settlement;
- contractual restrictions;
- Kansas governmental residual.

## 2. Firewalls

Do **not** infer Kansas availability from ticket face value.  
Do **not** classify multi-state obligations without evidence.  
Do **not** reconstruct the Round 2 PDF.

Prize obligations remain first claim. Multi-state prize-pool and contribution mechanics, if evidenced, may affect post-prize revenue, unavoidable cost, protected transition obligation, or residual availability — **UNKNOWN** until evidence.
