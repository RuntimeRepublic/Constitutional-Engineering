```text
CASH PROCEEDS ≠ FREE REVENUE
DEBT ISSUANCE IS NOT FREE WEALTH
DO NOT USE DEBT PROCEEDS TO SATISFY THE 90% TARGET WITHOUT LIABILITY TREATMENT
```

## 1. If any later mechanism uses debt

```text
DEBT ISSUER
→ CASH ASSET
+ DEBT LIABILITY.
```

CASH PROCEEDS ≠ FREE REVENUE.

## 2. If Rule-of-72 individual capital later acquires a public-debt asset

```text
INDIVIDUAL → FINANCIAL ASSET.
ISSUER → CASH + LIABILITY.
```

Do **not** use debt proceeds to artificially satisfy the 90% target without corresponding liability treatment. Do **not** select an ownership model, custodian, or public-debt instrument in this CWC.
