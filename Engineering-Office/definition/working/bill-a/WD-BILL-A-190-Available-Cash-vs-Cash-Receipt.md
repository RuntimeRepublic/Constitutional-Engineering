```text
CASH RECEIVED ≠ CASH AVAILABLE
LOTTERY BANK BALANCE ≠ AUTOMATICALLY AVAILABLE GOVERNMENTAL REVENUE
```

## 1. Distinction

**CASH RECEIPT** = money received.  
**AVAILABLE GOVERNMENTAL CASH** = money remaining after recognized waterfall claims, restrictions, and evidenced protected/unavoidable obligations.

A cash receipt may be encumbered, owed, restricted, or required for a valid prior claim.

## 2. Candidate chain

```text
CASH RECEIVED
→ CLAIM / RESTRICTION TEST
→ COST CLASSIFICATION
→ AVAILABLE-CASH DETERMINATION.
```

Do **not** treat the Lottery bank balance as automatically available governmental revenue.
