## 1. Human-intended engineering objective (Q-015 = A)

Controllable administration **≤ 10%** of the defined 90/10 engineering base.

If controllable administration is 9%, 8%, 7%, or lower, **no additional spending is authorized** merely because capacity remains under the 10% ceiling.

## 2. Operating principle

LOWEST PRACTICABLE CONTROLLABLE ADMINISTRATIVE COST subject to LAWFUL + SECURE + RELIABLE lottery operation.

## 3. What 10% is not

Not a budget entitlement. Not a mandatory spending floor. Not a required target expenditure. Not a finding about current Kansas Lottery administration. Not a statutory percentage. Not a certified achievable result.
