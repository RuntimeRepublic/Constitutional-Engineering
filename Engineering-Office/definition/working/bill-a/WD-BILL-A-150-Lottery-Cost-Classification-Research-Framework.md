## 1. Separation to be evidenced later

**Controllable administration:** costs that may be redesigned, automated, or reduced without making the lottery unsafe or unlawful.

**Unavoidable direct economic cost:** costs later evidenced as necessary to operate the lottery safely and lawfully (which may still be distinct from legally **protected** obligations).

## 2. Examples requiring later classification (not classified here)

Lottery personnel; claims processing; accounting; customer service; compliance; fraud detection; cybersecurity; retailer commissions; payment processing; gaming-system vendors; ticket production; MUSL / multi-state obligations; prize administration; marketing; facilities; technology; audit; legal; data/telemetry infrastructure.
