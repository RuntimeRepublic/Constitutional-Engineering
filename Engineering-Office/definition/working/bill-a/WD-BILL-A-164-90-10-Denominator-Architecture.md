## 2. Definition of the sides

**90 SIDE** = target maximum public / taxpayer-attributable value from the economic base remaining after:

PRIZES + RULE-OF-72 PARTITION + EVIDENCED UNAVOIDABLE DIRECT ECONOMIC COSTS.

**10 SIDE** = target maximum controllable administrative burden relative to **that same defined base**.

**90/10 BASE** =

```text
POST-PRIZE REVENUE
− RULE-OF-72 PARTITION
− EVIDENCED UNAVOIDABLE DIRECT ECONOMIC COSTS
= CANDIDATE 90/10 ENGINEERING BASE.
```
