## 1. Candidate Definition

An **UNAVOIDABLE DIRECT ECONOMIC COST** should generally require evidence that the cost is materially necessary to:

**A.** create, obtain, execute, settle, secure, or lawfully operate the lottery transaction or game;

**B.** satisfy a binding external obligation that cannot simply be removed by administrative redesign;

**C.** provide a necessary third-party economic function whose elimination would prevent or materially impair lawful lottery operation;

**or**

**D.** satisfy another demonstrated non-administrative requirement that remains necessary under the Bill A candidate architecture.

## 2. Insufficient grounds (not classification)

A cost **SHALL NOT** be classified unavoidable merely because:

- Kansas currently pays it;
- it appears in a current budget;
- it has historically existed;
- it is performed by current staff;
- it is embedded in an existing workflow;
- a current statute mentions it;
- a current vendor performs it;
- it has always been done that way.

## 3. Distinction

Unavoidable direct economic cost **≠** protected transition obligation (WD-BILL-A-178). Temporary contractual protection is **not** permanent economic necessity.

This is **candidate Definition language**. It is **not** an operative statutory definition and **not** a classification of any current Kansas Lottery expenditure.
