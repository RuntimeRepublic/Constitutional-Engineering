## 13. Mathematical distinction (required)

Under a **uniform** event-tax rate:

```text
TOTAL REVENUE ≈ TAXABLE DOLLAR BASE × TAX RATE
```

Transaction-size distribution does **not** by itself change total revenue if the same taxable dollars face the same rate. Distribution still matters for **visibility, administration, compliance, rounding, cash-flow, behavior, any future thresholds, incidence, and communications** (Senator Murphy’s practical question).
