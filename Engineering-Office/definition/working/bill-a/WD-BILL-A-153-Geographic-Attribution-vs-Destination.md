## 1. Attribution vs destination

**Attribution** = which taxpayer / geographic unit generated the governmental value.  
**Destination** = what authorized public purpose receives the value (here, primarily PT abatement/retirement of **available** dollars).

A county-attributed lottery dollar may later support an authorized USD, county, city, township, special district, debt transition, or other legitimate function. **No distribution percentages** are created here.

## 2. Geographic attribution planning Human Intent (this CWC)

This CWC Human-authorizes recording of the following planning concept for **attributable governmental lottery revenue**. WD-BILL-A-117 did not contain this split as a numbered Q-answer; 105-County Fund formula remains unresolved (LOT-G-014).

**Registered / identified Kansas purchaser:**

- 50% → county of sale  
- 50% → purchaser’s Kansas county of domicile  
- If the same county: effective attribution = **100%** to that county

**Anonymous / unregistered purchase:**

- 50% → county of sale  
- 50% → Kansas 105-County Fund

**Unresolved (do not invent):**

- 105-County Fund distribution formula  
- Identified non-Kansas purchaser treatment
