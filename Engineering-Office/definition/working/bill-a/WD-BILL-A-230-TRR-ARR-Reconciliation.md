TRR = Transitional Revenue Requirement for qualifying presently enforceable transition obligations (Q-008 = B). CURRENT SPENDING ≠ TRR. HISTORICAL SPENDING ≠ TRR. BUDGETED / APPROPRIATED / PROGRAM EXISTENCE / STAFFING ≠ automatic TRR.

ARR = Authorized Revenue Requirement for later Future-State engineering. TRR ≠ ARR. Neither is calculated.

NEW SPENDING → NBBF. Transition does not automatically grandfather new spending into protected status. NBBF admission procedure not invented. Four-month accountability remains high-level Human Intent.

ALL-IN three-layer architecture: TRANSITION → TRR; ACTIVE/ACCOUNTABILITY → NBBF-admitted spending; FUTURE → ARR.
