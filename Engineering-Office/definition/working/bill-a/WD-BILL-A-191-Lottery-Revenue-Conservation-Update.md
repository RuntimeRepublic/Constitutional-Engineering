## 2. Simultaneous-ownership ban

No dollar SHALL simultaneously be represented as:

**A.** Rule-of-72 individual capital;  
**B.** unavoidable direct economic cost;  
**C.** controllable administration;  
**D.** available governmental cash;  
**E.** property-tax abatement;  
**F.** another governmental destination

**unless** the record explicitly represents **sequential movement** of the same dollar rather than simultaneous ownership/use.

Forbidden: the same cash as 90-side available governmental cash **and** Rule-of-72 partition; the same cash in two destinations; noncash credit treated as a second cash position.
