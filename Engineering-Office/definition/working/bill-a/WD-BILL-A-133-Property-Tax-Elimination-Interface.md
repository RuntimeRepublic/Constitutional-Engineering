## 1. Controlling Human Intent

Every Kansas county eliminates property taxation. ALL-IN / ALL-OUT does **not** determine whether property tax survives. ALL-OUT is now **held**; PT→0 remains statewide.

Each county independently selects a **5-year or 7-year maximum runway**. Additional authorized ALL-IN revenue sufficiency may accelerate elimination. Qualifying seniors and qualifying disabled persons on fixed incomes remain first-priority classes where a transition remains. Eligibility mechanics remain unresolved.
