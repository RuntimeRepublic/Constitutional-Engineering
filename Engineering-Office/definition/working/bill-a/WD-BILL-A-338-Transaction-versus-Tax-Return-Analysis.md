Public KDOR products (annual report; monthly sales/use by county and NAICS) are **aggregate collections**, not receipt-size histograms.

Kansas retailers typically file **period sales/use tax returns** reporting **aggregated taxable sales** by jurisdiction for the filing period. That architecture often **cannot reconstruct exact receipt-size distribution** from tax-return totals alone.

CWC-CE-182 therefore asks KDOR to state the reporting unit: individual receipts; retailer-period aggregates; tax-return totals; POS information; marketplace/remote totals; or other.

If KDOR answers **NOT MAINTAINED** at receipt level, that is useful evidence, not a failed inquiry. Do not force an estimate in this cycle.
