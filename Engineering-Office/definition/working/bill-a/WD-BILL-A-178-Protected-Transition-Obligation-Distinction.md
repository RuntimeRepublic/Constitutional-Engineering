## 1. Distinction

Keep distinct:

**UNAVOIDABLE DIRECT ECONOMIC COST** (WD-BILL-A-165)  
and  
**PROTECTED TRANSITION OBLIGATION** (this file).

A contract may create a **temporary** protected obligation even if the underlying function could eventually be redesigned.

## 2. Model

```text
CURRENT CONTRACTUAL OBLIGATION
→ TRANSITION PROTECTION IF REQUIRED
→ EXPIRATION / LAWFUL MODIFICATION
→ FUTURE-STATE COST CLASSIFICATION.
```

Do **not** confuse temporary contractual protection with permanent economic necessity.

Current statutory destination ≠ automatic Future-State entitlement. Current statutory destination ≠ automatically available cash. Beneficiary audit (WD-BILL-A-157) remains required. This cycle does **not** determine beneficiary dispositions.

Bill A cannot lawfully impair protected obligations. That fact does **not** convert every current lottery expenditure into an unavoidable Future-State cost.
