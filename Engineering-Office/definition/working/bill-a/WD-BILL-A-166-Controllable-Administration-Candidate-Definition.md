## 1. Candidate Definition

**CONTROLLABLE ADMINISTRATION** should generally include costs arising from the governmental management, processing, supervision, reporting, staffing, workflow, support, or administrative execution of the lottery that can potentially be:

- eliminated;
- automated;
- consolidated;
- simplified;
- competitively sourced;
- technologically redesigned;
- reassigned;
- reduced;
- otherwise engineered downward

**without** destroying the lawful economic transaction or violating a protected obligation.

## 2. Dual firewall

Do **not** assume every employee or internal function is unnecessary.  
Do **not** assume current staffing levels are required.

Controllable administration is the candidate **10% side** of the 90/10 engineering target (WD-BILL-A-164). Classification of any specific function remains **UNKNOWN / RESEARCH REQUIRED** until the cost test (WD-BILL-A-167) is applied to evidence.
