## What is Bill A?

Bill A is an engineering project to design a **comprehensive Kansas tax-system replacement**. It is Human Engineering Intent and Definition work. It is **not** current Kansas law, **not** an introduced bill, and **not** proposed statutory text.

## What is an LOU?

A Letter of Understanding (LOU) is the controlled Engineering Definition document that records current Human Intent carefully enough to be examined, criticized, and improved **before** Human acceptance (HG-D1) and before any later specification or bill drafting.

For Bill A, that document is **LOU-004 Draft 1.12**.

## Why review before drafting a bill?

Because mistakes are cheaper to find in Definition than in statute. Public review is meant to expose wrong assumptions, legal conflicts, fiscal weaknesses, implementation problems, contradictions, unintended consequences, better alternatives, and missing evidence — **before** HG-D1.

## What is Draft 1.12?

It is the frozen public-review baseline Human-accepted **for review presentation** (PDFs visually accepted). It is still **not** HG-D1 accepted.

Incorrect assumptions; legal or constitutional problems; fiscal weaknesses; implementation and transition risks; contradictions; unintended consequences; better alternatives; missing evidence — especially where the package already marks matters **unresolved**.
