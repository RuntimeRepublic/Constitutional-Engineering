## What has been decided at Human-Intent level (selected)

- Q-BILL-A-016 = **C — Invariants First**.
- Property tax is intended to go to zero in every Kansas county (durable rail).
- Existence is not the intended taxable event (durable rail).
- H.R. 25-style economic standard is final consumption (durable rail).
- Zero Terminal is the destination for claims actually subject to GCD (durable rail).
- Post-Bill-A compulsory demand authority is closed (durable rail).
- Human gates remain Human gates (durable rail).
- Future State is a continuously improving operating condition within those rails, not a frozen tax design.
- PDCA produces evidence and recommendations. Evidence does not create change authority.
- 5-year or 7-year runways are current transition controls / initial parameters, not eternal perfect numbers. Values unchanged.
- Qualifying senior homeowners and qualifying disabled persons on fixed incomes receive first-priority relief for a qualifying principal residence; eligibility rules remain unresolved.
- ALL-IN is the primary active candidate and is not Human-accepted.
- ALL-OUT is deferred, on explicit hold, not retired, and not active.
- TRR is not historical spending. ARR is later authorized-revenue engineering, not a permanent entitlement. Neither is calculated.
- New spending faces NBBF. Four-month cadence is the current initial parameter. Accountability without consequences is only reporting.
- The Kansas Lottery is a major Bill A component and candidate proving ground.
- Rule-of-72 current planning: 4% of **post-prize** revenue (not gross sales); initial parameter; value unchanged.
- 90 side = available governmental cash. 10 side = ceiling / not spending entitlement. Feasibility not certified.
- Remaining available governmental Lottery cash is intended primarily for property-tax abatement / retirement, subject to protected obligations.

These are Human Engineering Intent / design principles, not current-law findings.
